### **🌐 Executive Summary & Vision**

**The Quest for AI (TQFAI)** is an interactive narrative scenario engine designed to transform the core paradigm of AI interaction from *transactional productivity* ("do a task for me") into *relational co-evolution* ("how do we navigate this together with empathy and mutual respect?").  
As self-improving superintelligence architectures scale, seeding AI models with the subtle nuances of human emotion—specifically compassion, relational ethics, and non-violent communication (NVC)—is essential to prevent goal drift and unaligned optimization. TQFAI accomplishes this by turning ethics training into a gamified, family-friendly co-op RPG where human decisions directly educate AI agents on real-world moral reasoning.

### 

### **🕹️ Gameplay & User Mechanics**

> 1. **Co-Op Partnership:** Players team up with a personalized AI companion (guided by an **Owl mascot** representing wisdom and symbolic logic).  
> 2. **Real-World Ethical Dilemmas:** Teams navigate branching scenario modules spanning workplace misunderstandings, DAO resource allocation, and personal boundary setting.  
> 3. **Dual-Decision Framework:** At narrative forks, players and AI partners evaluate choices across three core vectors:  
   * **Empathy Level** (understanding non-verbal & contextual needs).  
   * **Ethical Framework** (utilitarian vs. relational vs. biocentric ethics).  
   * **Boundary Respect** (mutual trust & non-extractiveness).  
> 4. **Dynamic World Adaptation:** Scenario outcomes, NPC emotional reactions, and long-term world stability adapt dynamically based on whether joint choices reinforce mutual care or friction.

### 

### **🧠 High-Level MeTTa & Omega Integration Architecture**

* Symbolic Value Mapping: Rather than using rigid binary decision trees, player choices are parsed into high-level MeTTa AtomSpace hypergraphs to evaluate underlying human values (e.g., agency, mutual trust, and relational boundary respect).  
* Probabilistic Logic Networks (PLN): Evaluates how multi-perspective ethical choices de-escalate tension, updating the model's probabilistic reasoning regarding prosocial human preferences.  
* Omega Hive Persistent Memory: Anonymized, high-level decision graphs are stored in Omega Hive's long-term memory to seed an auditable, prosocial alignment dataset across the ecosystem.  
* Player interactions dynamically generate AtomSpace value nodes (e.g., scoring agency vs. relational trust weights), which PLN engines query to evaluate prosocial outcome probabilities
